## How I work

- **Start with the user journey:** what someone is trying to accomplish and where they get stuck  
- **Design the structure:** clear nav, consistent templates, predictable naming  
- **Write for skimming:** headings that answer questions, short sections, examples early  
- **Make it maintainable:** docs-as-code when appropriate, lightweight governance, review workflows
